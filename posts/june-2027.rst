=====
Games
=====

A wide supply of 18xx games are commonly available, brought by
attendees and from local 18xx players' collections, but if there's
something you particularly want to play, bring it!  Several 18xx
designers commonly also bring their prototypes to play and discuss.
Feel free to use the `18PDX Discord <https://discord.gg/uw6as37qMJ>`__
to help plan and organise games.

See the galleries for some of the `games played in prior years
</galleries>`__.
